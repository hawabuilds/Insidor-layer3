# Sample images: credits

These photos stand in for post pictures in the phone app's **sample data** (the Narratives list, and the For you feed when the build is set to ). They are not the pictures of any real post, and the screens that show them carry the permanent "Sample data" notice.

Every image is **CC0 or Public Domain Mark**, found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org) and downloaded from its original host on 2026-10-11. Neither licence requires credit; it is given anyway. Each was centre-cropped to 400×400 and saved as JPEG. No image shows a recognisable person.
