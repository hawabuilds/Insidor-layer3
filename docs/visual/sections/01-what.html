01. What Insidor is

Memecoins are made out of things going viral. Every tool a trader uses today shows them a coin only after it exists and money is already moving into it. Insidor works one step earlier: we watch the internet, not the money, and show people the story before anyone has made a coin out of it.

The problem

Axiom, Photon and GMGN are all screens full of coins. They are good screens. But a coin can only appear on them once somebody has already created it, and by then the interesting part has happened. The first buyers are in. The chart already has a shape. What you are looking at is not an opportunity, it is the aftermath of one.

That has a blunt consequence. If you are consistently arriving after the coin exists, you are not early — you are the person the early buyers sell to. Being fast on those tools only makes you the quickest of the late.

The idea

Every one of those coins started as something on the internet: a clip, a photo, a sentence that made a lot of people laugh at the same time. That thing is public, it is free to look at, and it exists before the coin does. So instead of watching the money, we watch the thing the money will eventually chase. We scan social media, spot posts that are exploding right now, and put them in front of traders while there is still nothing to buy.

One story, one clock. Everyone else's product begins at 09:47, where the coin already exists. Ours begins at 09:13, where there is only a video of a ferry. The 34 minutes in between is the entire company.

Why it is defensible

Speed alone is not a moat — anyone can build something fast. What protects Insidor is that two assets get bigger every day the product runs, and a competitor starting tomorrow starts with none of either.

COMPOUND #1 · THE COMMUNITY
Their thesis history lives here

Traders post their reasoning on each story — why this one is real, why that one is nothing. Over months that becomes a public track record with their name on it.

People do not abandon a reputation they spent a year building. The writing is the lock-in, and we are not the ones who have to produce it.

COMPOUND #2 · THE RECORD
Which viral posts became coins that made money

Every story we surface, we keep: what it looked like as it climbed, whether a coin followed, and how that coin did. Nobody sells this. You cannot go back and ask what a post looked like twenty minutes ago — if it was not recorded live, it is gone.

A competitor can copy the screen in a fortnight. They cannot copy a year of yesterdays.

Who this is for

THE TRADER
Wants to be first, not fast

Already good at reading a chart, already has Axiom open. What they do not have is anything that fires before the chart exists. That is the only thing we sell them.

THE CREATOR
Wants to mint off a meme

Their whole job is finding the funny thing early. Today that means scrolling all day and hoping. Here the candidates arrive already sorted, with a name and ticker suggested.

THE NEWCOMER
Saw it on TikTok, wants in

No wallet, no jargon, no idea what a bonding curve is. They get a page that explains the story in one sentence and a button that works. Everything else stays out of the way.

Three people, one screen. The trader reads it for the timing, the creator for the raw material, the newcomer for the sentence at the top. Nothing on the page is written for an insider only.
