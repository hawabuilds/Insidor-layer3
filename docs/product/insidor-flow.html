Product flow · v1

The flow, expanded

Insidor watches TikTok and X for things going viral, tells you before crypto traders find out, shows you the coins people are making out of them, and lets you make the first one if nobody has.

This is the document the interface gets rebuilt from. Every screen has a named way in and a named way out. Nothing here is infrastructure — that stays deferred.

01

Why nothing correlated

One structural bug explains every symptom.

Root cause

The story and the coin are the same record

In every version so far, a viral post is a ticker. One object, one row, one card. That single decision is why the features would not connect:

  • There is nowhere to put a second coin, so “Buy $FERRY” had to pretend only one exists.
  • There is nowhere to put you — no holding, no position — so the product ended at the purchase.
  • There is nowhere to put what happened afterwards, so nothing could prove it works.

Split story from coin and the rest falls out on its own. A story is the spine. Coins hang off it, and so do you.

Everything below is downstream of that one split. The navigation gets simpler, not more complicated, because objects now have somewhere to live.

02

The loop

One path through the product. Everything else is a branch off this.

The rule that removes the ambiguity

The card's button is a pure function of how many coins exist. Zero → Make the coin. Exactly one → Buy $FERRY. Two or more → See the 3 coins. No card in the app can say “Buy” when more than one coin exists — not because the copy is careful, but because that state cannot be rendered.

03

Eight objects

Nothing else exists. If a feature does not touch one of these, it does not ship.

ObjectIn plain EnglishRelationshipsWhere you see it
StoryA thing going viral right now that people might make a coin out of. 1 post, 1 poster, 0–n coins, 0–1 main coin Trending card, Story page, Search, the band on every coin page, Following
PostThe actual video or tweet it came from. belongs to 1 story Card hero, Story page timeline. Its permalink is the proof — the lead-time claim is unfalsifiable without it.
PosterThe person who posted it. They get a cut whether they know it or not. 1 → n stories Card byline, Story page, the claim page — which is their entire product
CoinA coin somebody made out of a story. There can be more than one. n → 1 story, 1 maker, 0–n holdings Coin page, story's coin list, Search, Live rail, You
FollowYou told us to keep an eye on something. targets a story or a coin — never silently converts You → Following. Nine feeders, which fixes the empty watchlist.
HoldingWhat you own, what you paid, how you are doing. 1 coin, 1 story You → What you own, the position block on a coin page, the sell sheet
LaunchA coin you made. 1 coin You → What you made, plus a live earnings band on the coin page
PayoutMoney a coin owes somebody. 3 roles per coin: maker 50, poster 40, Insidor 10 Create form, You, the claim page
The number that carries the product

minutes_early_when_you_bought exists on every holding and appears nowhere in any build so far: “You got in 41 minutes before crypto traders did.” That is the emotional payload of the whole thing, and it is currently missing.

Note that minutes_early can be negative, and we show it. Roughly a third of real detections will be late. A track record with no left tail reads as marketing.

06

A story has four states

The prototype has one boolean, hasCoin. A screen cannot render what time it is if there is no clock.

StateThe words the user seesWhat causes itWhat the card does
Just spotted“Just spotted — nobody in crypto has posted about this yet”Detected under 15 minutes agoFull colour, accent line, sorts near the top
Spreading“Spreading — more people are posting about it every minute”Posts per 5 min rising, three checks in a rowFull colour, top of the feed
Cooling off“Cooling off — fewer people are posting about it now”Fallen below 60% of the busiest 5 minColour drains to ~65%, image shrinks to a thumbnail, sorts down, leaves after 2h
Over“Over” plus one sentence on what happenedBelow 15% of peak for a full hourLeaves Trending. Appears under Finished in Search and in Following

The same four words render identically in six places: the Trending card, the Search row, the band on every coin page, the Following row, the What-you-own subtitle, and the Live rail. A story that never got a coin is not hidden — it becomes a Finished row reading “Nobody ever made a coin from this one. Sometimes that is the right call.”

07

Seven journeys

If a screen is not reachable by one of these, it should not exist.

J1Never bought anything — first three minutes

Lands on Trending from a friend's link. No wallet box, no tour, no modal. Header: “Insidor watches the internet and tells you what is about to blow up. Right now: 412 stories, and we are catching them about 31 minutes before crypto traders do.” One card down: image, byline, post, Why it is spreading, minutes early, one button. The smallest first action is Follow — free, no wallet.

J2Follow a story with no coin, come back when one exists

Follows from No coin yet in Search. Two hours later, Trending shows a While you were away band: “1 story you follow got a coin. Wrong Name is now $CUPS.” Lands on the coin page. The story follow stays alive, so it fires again if a second coin appears.

J3Three coins exist — pick one

The card cannot say Buy. It says See the 3 coins and lands on the story page. Three ranked rows, top marked BIGGEST, each showing exactly three facts in the same order: money in it, people who own it, how long ago it was made — then the safety line in words. No market cap, no liquidity, no volume in Simple.

J4Nobody made a coin, so you make the first

“Nobody has made a coin from this story. You would be first. It costs about 2 dollars and takes 30 seconds. You keep half the fees from every trade, forever.” Two steps. The story is fixed at the top and cannot be changed.

J5After the buy — check on it, then get out

The half of the product that does not exist today. “You have put in $141.20. It is worth $179.60. You are up $38.40.” Each row's subtitle is the thing no other trading app can say: “Last Horn — the story is cooling off.” Sell / Buy more / See coin.

J6The person who filmed the video

Has never heard of crypto. Gets a deliberately flat message: “Someone made a coin based on your Norwegian cup video. You have earned $49.50 from it so far. It is yours whether or not you do anything.” No exclamation marks, no emoji — those read as a scam to this reader.

J7Coming back the next day

The order is the design: your money, your earnings, your follows, then the world. A dismissible band, not a modal and not a separate screen.

08

The correlation fixes

Each of these closes a specific hole you spotted.

  • FIXBuy is a function of a count. The ambiguous state is not resolved by copy — it cannot be rendered.
  • FIXStories never buy. Coins buy. Buying happens in exactly two places: a coin page, and a row in What you own. Nowhere else.
  • FIXThe main coin is the one with the most money in it. Not a weighted score — a rule you can say out loud in half a sentence. If the biggest is under 2× the second, we pick nothing and say so.
  • FIXWe never say “pick” and never put Insidor's name on a coin. The label is BIGGEST, which is a fact, with the rule printed underneath.
  • FIXNine feeders into the watchlist, including automatic follows on every buy and every coin you make. Nothing you touch fails to appear in You.
  • FIXEvery live-rail row has a named destination. Trades, new coins and viral posts each land somewhere specific, and each header has a See all.
  • FIXLead time is one number owned by the story. Everything else is arithmetic on it, expressed in words. Three competing green minute chips was your complaint restated as a feature.
  • FIXThe claim is verified by two public permalinks, not by trusting us — the original post and the first crypto post, both somebody else's timestamps.
  • FIXBeing late is published in the same feed, in grey, in the same slot. “Crypto traders got here 6 minutes before us.”
  • FIXFollowing a story never silently becomes following a coin. Migrating it would destroy what you actually asked for.
09

Deliberately not building

The full design produced far too much product. These are cut on purpose, so they do not creep back.

  • CUTA fourth and fifth tab. Following became a section of You; the archive became a filter chip plus one pinned card.
  • CUTAll onboarding. No welcome panels, no interest picker, no forced risk screen. You land on the feed. The proof card is item two.
  • CUTThe alerts inbox. None of the seven journeys ever opened it. While you were away answers “what did I miss” better.
  • CUTPractice money. The most painful cut — it put fake money in the same list as real money, separated by a corner chip. Follow already gives a wallet-free action.
  • CUTThe command palette. A search box wearing a keyboard shortcut. Cmd K now just focuses the search field.
  • CUTThe weighted consensus score (40% liquidity / 30% volume / 20% holders…). Four proposals produced four incompatible formulas, which is proof the decision was not ready.
  • CUTMarket cap, liquidity, volume, slippage, mint authority, LP, bonding curve, rug, bag — as words, in Simple mode. Every one appears in the current prototype. Pro may show the numbers; Simple never shows the vocabulary.
  • CUTNine story states and eleven coin states. Twenty status words before a beginner has bought anything. Four and four.
  • CUTNew Pairs as a destination. It was never a destination, it was a sort. It survives as a chip and a rail mode.
  • CUTInsidor holding a non-crypto person's money. It contradicts the one sentence we tell everyone else: your coins live in your wallet, not with us.
  • CUTBuying your own coin ahead of everyone inside the create form. The one screen that would make a beginner correctly ask whether they are running a scam.
  • CUTGraduation badges and confetti. A celebration on an event a beginner cannot parse, contradicting the no-celebration rule the rest of the product depends on.
What Pro mode is now

Exactly four things: adds numeric columns to tables, turns the chart into candles, expands the safety line into the full checklist, exposes slippage on the buy sheet. It never moves a button, never adds a screen, and never shows a state Simple cannot see.

10

Yours to decide

Real forks. I picked a default for each so the spec is complete, but these are product calls, not design calls.

  • ?Main coin = most money in it, or most owners? I picked money, because it decides whether you can actually sell later.
  • ?Does Insidor keep its 10% when the poster never claims their 40%? Currently the poster's money sits untouched forever and we still take our cut.
  • ?Can anyone make a coin from any story, or only ones we detected? Right now the form is only reachable from a story, which is what keeps creation coherent — but it also means you cannot make a coin about something we missed.
  • ?On a takedown, do we hide the coin only, or the story too? I specced coin only, so the public record keeps its lead-time claim.
  • ?Does following require an account? I assumed no sign-in, so a first visit can do something free — which means follows on a laptop are invisible on a phone.
  • ?Push notifications, ever? Everything is in-app until the user opts in. Push is the strongest retention lever in a market that moves in minutes.
  • ?Does profit or loss ever appear in the Trending feed? I kept money inside You and coin pages only.
  • ?Is 2× the right threshold for refusing to name a main coin? Higher is more honest but leaves beginners stuck.
