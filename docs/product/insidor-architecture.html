Insidor: detection to coin

A Solana web app that finds viral moments before crypto traders do, lets you buy the coin that exists or create the first one, and pays a share of fees to whoever made the original post.

This document is the result of two independent research passes: an adversarial fact-check of 120 extracted claims against 24 primary sources, and an eight-discipline engineering design that was then reviewed for contradictions. Both passes found showstoppers. They are in section 0, not buried.

The verdict

Three findings survived adversarial verification and each one independently threatens the product as specified. Read these before anything else.

Showstopper 1 · the headline mechanic has no trustless implementation

Routing a fee share to a poster identified only by a social handle, who never consented and has no wallet, cannot currently be done trustlessly on Solana.

  • Bags exposes GET /token-launch/fee-share/wallet/v2 which resolves a handle to a wallet [9] — but its documentation is silent on what happens for a handle that has never onboarded. That single undocumented behaviour decides the feature.
  • PumpPortal's collectCreatorFee harvests only to the caller's own wallet, and on the pump.fun pool it sweeps all tokens at once [10].
  • Token-2022 transfer fees are withheld on the destination account by deliberate design to preserve parallelism, and require a project-held withdraw authority to collect [11].

Every verified path is custodial. That converts your marquee feature from a smart-contract problem into an unclaimed-funds and money-transmission problem. It is a legal question with an engineering consequence, and it must be answered before Phase 5.

Showstopper 2 · platform policy may forbid the mechanic outright

On 15 January 2026, X revoked API access for "InfoFi" applications — apps attaching financial rewards to posting [4][20]. Insidor routes money to posters. The distinction we are relying on is that the payout triggers on trading volume, never on posting, which is structurally a royalty rather than a bounty on amplification.

That distinction is ours, not X's. The verification pass rated this finding low confidence precisely because nobody has read the mechanic against the actual Developer Agreement text [19] rather than secondhand reporting. Get counsel to read it before building on X.

Showstopper 3 · TikTok is not available, on two independent grounds

A for-profit entity is categorically ineligible for TikTok's Research API under its own eligibility criteria and binding Research Tools ToS [2]. Separately, it runs roughly 48 hours behind — useless for a product measured in minutes. The Commercial Content API is regulator-gated; the Display API carries no discovery data; Instagram's hashtag search is capped at 30 hashtags per user per rolling 7 days.

There is no lawful first-party discovery API for TikTok or Instagram. The architecture below therefore treats X as the sensor for every platform and non-X sources as hydration targets only.

And one structural finding from the design review

Eight independent specialists designed the create path in exhaustive detail. Not one of them designed story-to-existing-coin matching — mapping a detected story to a mint that already exists. That is the buy path, it is the majority of trade volume, and it is the thing the product promises first. It is now assigned explicitly in Phase 4.

01

What the product is

Insidor watches social media for things going viral that have nothing to do with crypto, surfaces them before crypto traders find them, and lets a user either buy the coin that already exists or create the first one. When creating, the coin's name, ticker and image are generated from the source post. Creator fees split three ways: 50% creator, 40% original poster, 10% Insidor.

The one number

Lead time — minutes between Insidor detecting a story and the first crypto mention of it. It is the value proposition, the share hook, and the only claim that must be provable to a sceptic without trusting us.

The object model

Eight objects. Story and Coin are separate: one Story has 0..n Coins, a Coin belongs to exactly one Story. Everything else follows from that split.

Ingestion

Rust + tokio gateway, Redpanda bus, Postgres + TimescaleDB, Cloudflare R2 archive.

The finding that inverts the obvious design

The instinct is "official X stream as the licensed spine, reseller as the volume lane." The roles must invert. Filtered stream is not sold on pay-per-use — it starts at Pro (~$5,000/mo), which at 1k DAU is 3× the entire ingestion budget. But GET /2/tweets batch reads are pay-per-use at $0.005/read with same-UTC-day dedup.

So: reseller websocket is the stream spine, official X is the read spine. Official filtered stream turns on at Pro only above ~30k DAU — a config flag, not a rewrite.

Read-routing break-even
n* = C_official_per_post_per_utc_day / C_reseller_per_read = $0.005 / $0.00015 = 33.3 polls per post per UTC day
Poll a post more than 33×/day → official (flat, dedup makes it free after the first). Fewer → reseller. The hot lane runs 96 polls/day, the cold lane 12. This one number governs roughly 70% of the ingestion bill.
Assumption that must be killed in week 1 — cost $0.02

The entire routing model assumes X's same-UTC-day dedup is a billing dedup, not a response cache. If it caches responses, dense re-polling returns stale public_metrics and the velocity signal dies entirely.

Test: read one high-velocity post twice 90s apart in the same UTC day; assert like_count differs; then GET /2/usage/tweets and assert usage delta is 1, not 2.

X is the sensor for every platform

Since no lawful discovery API exists for TikTok or Instagram, X rules match outbound links — url:"tiktok.com/@", url:"instagram.com/reel" — and the URL is then hydrated through each platform's own public oEmbed. Post.platform is tiktok with a VIA_X_LINK provenance bit set. Anything viral enough to be worth a coin gets cross-posted to X within minutes.

Two hard constraints on the timing story

  • ~20s rule activation floor. Rules mutate live without reconnecting [1], but "most rule additions take effect about 20 seconds or less." You cannot react to a brand-new term — you must already be listening.
  • Semantic matching is Enterprise-only. The embedding: operator matches by meaning rather than keyword, but requires Enterprise plus an Embedding tier entitlement [1]. Baseline rule design must assume keyword and structural matching only.

Media waterfall

Five tiers, fixed card height across all five, so there is never a broken image:

  1. media.url photo — free, in the stream payload
  2. preview_image_url for video — free, no ffmpeg needed, X generates the still server-side
  3. og:image of the first outbound link — own fetch, free
  4. Image from the highest-scored reply — capped at 5, gated behind HOT ≥ p95, since at $0.005/read this is the most expensive operation in the product
  5. Deterministic typographic plate keyed on hash(story_id) — same story always looks the same, which is itself a provenance property
05

Virality detection

Rust stream workers scoring in-process against Dragonfly; LightGBM lambdarank for coefficient fitting on ClickHouse.

Correction to something stated earlier in this project

X's 2026 open-sourced ranker [5] gives the shape of a scorer — a linear weighted sum of predicted action probabilities, multiplicatively attenuated — but ships zero numeric coefficients, and has deleted SimClusters, TwHIN and real-graph entirely. The widely-circulated 2023 weights (favorite 0.5, retweet 1.0, reply 13.5, reply-engaged-by-author 75.0) cannot be attributed to it. Verification also killed the claim of a specific 15-action Phoenix probability set.

Consequence: Insidor must fit its own coefficients. The 2023 weights are usable only as a prior, never as a citation.

Action weight, derived not borrowed
w_a = C / p_a where p_a = platform base rate of action a per impression C = 0.0075, fixed by anchoring X repost to w = 1.0
The insight behind X's published weights is that weight is the inverse of rarity. Deriving w_a from our own observed base rates reproduces that logic without depending on numbers we cannot verify, and it generalises across platforms where base rates differ wildly.
Weighted engagement and rate
E_w(p,t) = SUM_a [ w_a * n_a(p,t) ] + (w_complete / D_sec) * total_watch_seconds(p,t) R(p,t) = E_w(p,t) / max(I(p,t), 1)
n_a = cumulative count of action a; D_sec = video duration; I = impressions (impression_count on X, play_count on TikTok). The watch-time term is what makes TikTok comparable to X at all.
Robust scale — why median and MAD, not mean and stddev
sigma_hat = max( 1.4826 * MAD, 0.5 * (Q90 - Q50) / 1.2816, sigma_floor_cohort ) MAD = median(|x - median(x)|)
1.4826 is the Gaussian consistency constant; 1.2816 is the 90th-percentile z. A viral post is the outlier you are measuring — using mean and standard deviation lets the event you are detecting corrupt its own baseline. This is Twitter's own Seasonal Hybrid ESD reasoning [8], which substitutes median for mean and MAD for stddev.

Burst detection

Kleinberg's automata [7] are fully specified and directly implementable. Two variants matter, and the distinction is the one most implementations get wrong:

  • A* operates over raw inter-arrival gaps. Good, but it is a batch/windowed dynamic program — not a streaming detector.
  • B* operates over windowed counts, which is the realistic ingestion shape, and produces a baseline-normalised burst weight that ranks competing viral moments against each other. That ranking is exactly what a feed needs.

Verification killed the specific claim that Kleinberg's cost recurrence is "identical in form to Viterbi" — implement from the paper, not from that description.

The deliberate inversion — the whole product rests on it

X applies a 0.75 multiplier to out-of-network posts and gates them behind social proof. Insidor inverts it: small accounts with no second-degree connections get a boost, because that is exactly where memecoin alpha lives and exactly what the platform is structurally worst at surfacing.

The review found this specified four different ways spanning 8× at 100 followers — the precise band where Sybil farms live. One Omega, owned by detection, version-locked to the security purity gate. Everything else is dead code.

Clustering and the auto-namer

Cohere embed-v4.0 at 1024-d Matryoshka, stored as halfvec(1024) in Postgres 17 + pgvector 0.8 with a partitioned HNSW index over hot stories only.

The decisive reason for embed-v4 is that it is natively interleaved text and image in one space — a TikTok still and an X caption about the same moment land near each other without a separate fusion step. Cost is negligible: $16/month at 500k DAU [16].

Story assignment score
score(p,s) = 0.68*cos(e_p, c_s) + 0.14*J(E_p, E_s) Jaccard over extracted entities + 0.08*host_overlap + 0.06*media_match + 0.04*exp(-dt_min/240) - 0.20*lang_mismatch*(1-cos)
e_p = L2-normalised embedding, c_s = story centroid. Assign if score exceeds threshold, else spawn a provisional story under an advisory lock keyed on the nearest centroid — which kills the concurrent-duplicate-spawn race.
Centroid update
alpha = max(0.02, 1/(n+1)) c_new = (1-alpha)*c_old + alpha*e_p , then c_new /= ||c_new||
The 0.02 floor stops a large story's centroid from freezing, so a story that mutates (a meme that acquires a new catchphrase) can still follow its own drift.

The auto-namer

Four branches in priority order, derived from 36 real cases. It shows its reasoning, because visible reasoning is what makes a generator feel intelligent rather than arbitrary.

BRANCH A  proper noun            confidence 85-95
  Does an animate subject have a name in text, caption or comments?
  The ticker IS the name, unmodified.  Nazgul -> $NAZGUL
  Five of the six largest animal conversions follow this with zero creativity.
  If this hits, STOP.

BRANCH B  stressed catchphrase noun      60-80
  Take the single most-shouted word, never the whole phrase.
  "hawk tuah" -> $HAWK    "chicken jockey" -> $JOCKEY

BRANCH C  deliberate misspelling         45-65
  Phonetic, deliberately wrong description of what is in frame.
  a dog wearing a hat -> $WIF     (the misspelling is load-bearing)

BRANCH D  object noun                    40-60
  Most distinctive concrete object.  Kool-Aid pineapple -> $JARS

HARD BLOCKS - never proposed
  - initialisms when any concrete noun exists
      $MVG sat on a 4-billion-impression event and died while
      the word "gorilla" was right there
  - numbers            unsearchable, collide with everything
  - registered brands where the joke is at the brand's expense
  - the creator's own real name

Critical input most implementations miss: top comments and quote-posts. "dogwifhat" was named by a replier, not the poster. "just a chill guy" was a caption someone else attached. The crowd usually names the thing before the product does.

07

On-chain

Launch on Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve (dbcij3LWUppWqq96dh6gJWwBifmcGfLSB5D4DuSMaqN), reached in Phase 1 through the Bags public API because its Fee Share v2 program already ships handle-to-wallet resolution [9].

This is where showstopper 1 lives

Bags takes a protocol cut before any creator-side split, so a fee-claimer's basis-points share applies to the creator half only — bounding what you can actually pay a poster at roughly half the naive figure. The "40%" in the UI is 40% of the creator leg, not 40% of all fees, and the product copy must say so.

Verification also killed the claims that splits must sum to exactly 10,000 bps and that fee-share config is immutable after launch. Do not design against those until re-verified.

Provenance — the one component that cannot be rented

An append-only, ed25519-signed, blake3-chained ledger written at detection time, before any coin exists, Merkle-anchored hourly to Solana in a single SPL memo instruction.

Ledger and anchor
leaf_hash = blake3(0x00 || canonical_cbor(record)) invariant : record[n].prev_hash == leaf_hash(record[n-1]) node(L,R) = blake3(0x01 || L || R) odd level duplicates last proof_len = ceil(log2(n_leaves)) anchor_cost = 5000 * n_sigs + (cu_limit * price_microlamports) / 1e6 = 5000 + (16000 * 25000)/1e6 = 5400 lamports ~ $0.0012 per anchor
At hourly anchoring that is about $60/year — and it is the only thing in the system that makes "we saw it first" checkable by a sceptic with no dependency on us. Ship it in Phase 1; every hour it is not running is permanently unrecoverable.

Execution

  • Jito bundles for create + first buy atomically, tip from getTipFloor. A one-click launch off a public feed is a sniper magnet by construction [12].
  • Jupiter for swap routing with integrator fees and pre-created referral token accounts [14].
  • Helius LaserStream gRPC for mint and trade events [13], behind an internal stream interface so the vendor is swappable.

Data model and spine

One PostgreSQL 18 primary is the entire spine: system of record, job queue via SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, vector store via pgvector 0.8 HNSW, and realtime transport via logical replication consumed by a Go fan-out service.

The decisive reason is that Insidor's write load is set by admitted stories, not raw posts — the admission controller holds that at a constant rate by design. That is a Postgres-shaped workload for a long time.

Tape hash chain — clients can detect gaps and tampering
h(0) = sha256("insidor.tape.v1") h(n) = sha256( h(n-1) || u64be(seq_n) || frame_id_n || u8(kind_n) || subject_id_n || u64be(ts_micros_n) || sha256(canonical_json(d_n)) ) prefix-visibility invariant: for any MVCC snapshot S, visible(S) = { r in tape : r.seq <= W(S) }
Holds because exactly one session assigns seq. A client that reconnects can prove it missed nothing.
Velocity and acceleration
v(p,t) = ( e_score(t) - e_score(t - dt) ) / (dt/60) e-units per minute a(p,t) = ( v(p,t) - v(p, t-dt) ) / (dt/60)
dt = poller interval at that age. Acceleration — the second derivative — is what catches the ripple rather than the wave, and it is a sortable column in Pro.
09

Client

React 19 + Vite 6 as a pure client-rendered SPA, with a small Cloudflare Worker serving crawler-only OG HTML for share cards.

No Next.js or RSC: the app is a persistent-WebSocket authenticated terminal where every byte of state is client state, so server components buy nothing and cost complexity. The Worker exists solely so a shared link unfurls — and it renders from a signed lead receipt, so a shared card can never carry a stale number.

ArrivalScheduler — why click targets never move under a finger
insertY = (rank - firstVisible)*ROW_H - (scrollTop mod ROW_H) safe = (now - lastPointerAt > GUARD_TTL_MS) OR (insertY > pointerY + GUARD_RADIUS_PX) interactionLocked(now) = (now - lastPointerDownAt < 600) OR (now - lastWheelAt < 220) OR (now - lastTouchMoveAt < 220) OR |scrollVelocity| > 40 px/s OR armedTicket != null
This is the single largest contributor to the chaotic feeling in competing terminals. Inserting above a reading user is the cardinal sin; the scheduler makes it structurally impossible.

Wallets: Privy embedded wallets plus Wallet Standard behind one WalletPort interface, so a user who has never held crypto can start, and a power user can bring their own. Insidor never custodies funds — which is exactly why showstopper 1 is dangerous, since the PosterVault escrow is the one place that principle bends.

Security and anti-abuse

Postgres as a constraint-enforcement plane — the anti-manipulation rules are UNIQUE constraints and CHECKs, not model outputs.

The finding that saves an ML project

Research shows per-account bot scores do not correlate well with coordinated liking behaviour. So structural limits beat classifiers: one contribution per author per story per 30-minute window, enforced as a database UNIQUE constraint. O(1), no model, no training set, and it structurally defeats engagement pods. Ship this before any bot classifier.

Sublinear Sybil damping
S_eff = SUM over clusters c of ( n_c ^ alpha ), alpha = 0.35 1 account -> 1.00 100 accounts -> 5.01 1000 accounts -> 11.2
Buying 1000 accounts buys 11× the influence of one, not 1000×. It makes farming economically pointless rather than technically impossible, which is the only defence that holds.
Coordination edge — machine-checkable, not vibes
E(a,b) = 1 iff |S_a & S_b| >= 5 AND J_w(a,b) >= 0.55 AND median |t_a(s) - t_b(s)| <= 180s AND |S_a & S_b| / min(|S_a|,|S_b|) >= 0.40
Two accounts are coordinating if they hit the same 5+ stories, with high weighted Jaccard, within 3 minutes of each other, and that overlap dominates at least one of their histories.

Burstiness (Goh-Barabási) B = (sigma_tau - mu_tau)/(sigma_tau + mu_tau) over inter-arrival times separates organic spread (bursty, B → 1) from scripted engagement (regular, B → -1).

The $42,000/month coin-flip

Ingestion forces X Enterprise at 500k DAU by counting post-days (7.6M reads/month, blowing the 2M pay-per-use cap). Backend says Enterprise is never triggered by counting distinct posts — a 25× discrepancy and a $42,000/month swing that nobody had noticed. Post-day accounting is the only arithmetically consistent one; route hot re-polls to the reseller and hold Enterprise in reserve.

What the review found

Eight specialists designed independently. A principal-engineer pass then hunted for contradictions. It found twelve, plus seven responsibilities nobody claimed.

Contradictions

  • 01Four message buses, no bridge. Ingestion and SRE build Redpanda. Detection and clustering consume NATS. Backend rejects both for Postgres logical replication. The missing bridge is on the critical path and appears in no cost model.
  • 02Three key spaces on the same objects. post_id is a namespaced string, a u128, and a uuid depending on the layer. Any join across them is broken.
  • 03Age decay specified three ways, disagreeing 12× at the floor. 0.6+0.4*2^(-t/360) vs 0.05+0.95*2^(-t/45) vs 2^(-t/45) with no floor.
  • 04The out-of-network inversion has four formulas spanning 8× at 100 followers — and the Sybil pricing is calibrated against the weakest one.
  • 05Poll cadence off by 6× — 60s vs 10s — and the hot-lane cost model rests on the higher figure.
  • 06UTC-day dedupe accounting differs 25×, swinging $42,000/month.
  • 07Protocol mismatch at the client boundary. Backend serves SSE over HTTP/2 with Last-Event-ID gap-fill; the client consumes WebSocket MessagePack.
  • 08TxGuard hard-blocks every Phase 1 launch and has no override path by design, because it decodes the split at an offset in a program that will not exist yet.
  • 09IP risk blocks launches in two layers and is forbidden from blocking in two others — on the exact Chill Guy scenario the feature exists for.
  • 10Coin image policy is mutually exclusive — source crop vs derived render — and both sides advance their choice as the legal defence.
  • 11Lead-time t0 has two contradictory definitions, and one of them is inflatable in the flattering direction. Use both endpoints on our own clock so measurement error cancels rather than accumulates.
  • 12Revenue disagrees 10× on creator fee (1% vs 0.1%) — which flips the seed tier between viable and dead.

Unowned responsibilities

  • !Story-to-existing-coin matching — the entire buy side, and the majority of trade volume.
  • !The Holding object — nobody owns reconciling on-chain balances with position rows, or cost basis and PnL.
  • !The Follow object — appears twice in eight designs, owned by none.
  • !The creator 50% and treasury 10% claim paths. The poster's 40% leg is designed in extreme depth; the other two legs are not designed at all.
  • !Broadcast ingestion — three layers budget ASR for it, zero build an adapter.
  • !The Pro-mode query language — half the declared UI surface, referenced by three layers, specified by none.

Legal and compliance

Right of publicity

Routing fees to a creator's handle without consent is state-law governed with no federal statute, and consent is the primary defence [23]. Mitigations must ship in the same release as the split, never after: a permanent opt-out registry, a "not created or endorsed by" disclaimer on every origin coin, and per-source rate limits so one viral post cannot spawn 200 dilutive launches.

The Chill Guy precedent

Phillip Banks announced DMCA action against unauthorised merchandise and "shitcoins" [22]; the token fell roughly 54% in two hours. Enforcement stalled because the deployer was anonymous and the token permissionless. The lesson is not that takedowns work — it is that IP risk is a price-moving, tradeable signal, which is why it is a first-class column in Pro mode.

Front-end registration exposure

SEC staff issued broker-dealer registration guidance for certain user interfaces in April 2026 [21]. A fee-charging front end that suggests an asset and offers one-click deployment is the exact profile that undermines a neutral-software defence. Concrete mitigations: present three suggestions and never one recommendation; never rank by predicted price performance; keep every transaction user-signed; retain no upgrade authority; route through a third-party launchpad rather than operating a curve.

Scraping posture

A federal court held that the Copyright Act preempts X's web-scraping claims [24], which is favourable on one axis. But the risk that kills a live-feed product is not a lawsuit — it is a Tuesday morning when the feed goes dark. The reseller sits in the hiQ posture (logged-in, contractually liable), not the Meta v. Bright Data safe harbour. Keep the licensed lane running even when it is not the cheapest.
