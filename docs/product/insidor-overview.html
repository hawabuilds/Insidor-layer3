Catch the play before it’s a coin.

Insidor watches social media for things going viral, surfaces them before crypto traders find out, and lets you either buy the coin that already exists or create the first one if nobody has.

Solana memecoin terminal · Narrative-first discovery · Buy early or mint first
01 — THE PREMISE

Every memecoin starts as a viral moment

A ferry captain’s last horn. A frog on a porch. A furious baby hippo. The coin is always downstream of the story — and the story is where the edge lives.

  • Competitors start at the coin. By the time Axiom or Photon shows you a token, you’re racing ten thousand people to something that already exists.
  • Insidor starts at the story. You see the viral moment 20–90 minutes before it becomes a coin. That head start is the entire product.
  • The one number that sells it: how early you were. It is the value proposition, the share hook, and the thing that must be provable.
02 — WHO IT’S FOR

Three people, one screen

  • The trader who wants to be first, not fast — catch the narrative before crypto Twitter does.
  • The creator who spots a meme and wants to mint the coin off it and earn the fees.
  • The normie who saw something blow up on TikTok and wants in — without needing to understand rugs, slippage or bonding curves.
03 — THE CORE FORK

Everything routes to one of two actions

A viral moment either already has coins, or it doesn’t. Every surface in the product funnels you to exactly one next step — never an ambiguous one.

A coin already exists
Buy the best one

The board shows its market cap. You go straight to the token page and trade.

No coin exists yet
Create the first

The board shows a Create button. You mint it off the story before anyone else.

The primary button everywhere is a pure function of that count: Buy $TICKER when one exists, Create Coin when none does. The ambiguous “buy which one?” state is never rendered.

04 — THE JOURNEY, BEGINNING TO END

From landing to earning

1

Land

You arrive on Trending — a ranked leaderboard of viral narratives, densest information first, like a token screener but for stories. The right rail streams live activity so the place feels alive the second you land.

2

Scan

Read the board top to bottom: rank, thumbnail, story, post snippet, views, gain, age, and either a market cap or a Create. Toggle Narratives ⇄ Tokens to flip between “what’s happening” and “what can I buy right now.” Or watch the Viral rail — posts arriving one by one with platform badge and media, before they even hit the board.

3

Judge

Click a story → the narrative modal: the real source post, the virality stat grid, a Google-Trends momentum curve, and a plain-English verdict line — “momentum established” versus “window narrowing.” This is the five-second “is this real, and am I early or late?” screen.

4

Decide

The modal ends in one button — Buy $HORN if a coin exists, Create coin if none does. Not sure? Search filters the token board to that story’s tickers, so you buy the real one instead of minting a duplicate.

5a

Buycoin exists

Land on the token trading page: contract address, TradingView chart, price with 5m / 1h / 24h. A real Buy / Sell panel — amount presets, slippage, priority, “you receive,” price impact, fees, you sign in your own wallet. Safety checks right there — mint revoked, freeze revoked, LP burned — so a normie can’t get rugged blind. Trades / Holders tabs below, and a View narrative → link back to the story that proves why this coin exists and how early you are.

5b

Createno coin yet

The auto-namer turns the post into a proposed ticker, name and image — built from how memes are actually named — with alternatives and a regenerate. You see the cost, the fee split and the safety defaults before signing. Then it’s live, and it’s a row on the board for everyone.

6

Hold & return

What you own lives in your portfolio. Watch (☆) follows stories that haven’t minted yet and pulls you back the moment they do. The story keeps living on the board until it cools off and joins the record of what already played out.

07 — THE ECONOMICS

The growth loop is built into the fees

Creator 50% Original poster 40% Insidor 10%
Viral post → coin → poster earns → poster amplifies → more posts surfaced
  • Trading fees split three ways, on-chain, at launch.
  • The original poster earns whether or not they know — so when their meme becomes a coin, they have a reason to shill it, which pulls new eyes back to Insidor.
  • That is the flywheel: the people whose content drives the platform are paid to promote the platform.

Cooling off

09 — THE HONEST EDGE

It only works if two claims hold

“We saw it first,” and “being early actually paid.” Both are shown, not asserted.
  • The lead time is provable — the source post and the first crypto mention are both public, timestamped, and anyone can check them.
  • The misses stay on the record next to the wins — a track record with no losses reads as a casino ad. Honesty is the moat.
