A STORY is the set of posts. A SUBJECT is what they are about. They are not the same
thing and the distinction is the most common way this task goes wrong.

Worked contrasts:

  posts: several videos of the same cat knocking a glass off a table, filmed by
         different people, some using the same sound
  story: "cat knocks glass off table"
  subject: the cat, if the posts identify it as the same cat by name or appearance;
           otherwise there is NO subject — the posts share a genre, not a thing.

  posts: a screenshot of one sentence someone wrote, reposted with reactions
  story: "people reacting to the sentence"
  subject: the sentence itself. Name it by quoting it, shortened.

  posts: people filming themselves doing a dance to one song
  story: "the dance"
  subject: usually NONE. A dance and a sound are a format. A subject would be the
           person who originated it, but only if the posts are about that person
           rather than merely using their sound.

  posts: a character from a game appearing in edits by many accounts
  subject: the character. Name it as the posts name it, not as the publisher does.

News, events and arguments are the commonest posts with a clear story and no subject.
Each pair below is the same name twice: once as news, once as a subject. Learn the
difference, not the names.

  posts: reports and reactions about Biden tapes on classified documents
  story: a news event
  subject: NONE. Not coinable.
  posts: Biden edited into the same joke by many accounts, memed AS a character
  subject: Biden. Coinable.

  posts: "Messi's team lost the final", with reactions
  story: a sports result
  subject: NONE. Not coinable.
  posts: a dog that ran onto the pitch during Messi's final, clipped and joked about by
         many accounts
  subject: the dog (PITCHDOG), not Messi. Coinable.

  posts: "Therapy industry criticised as ineffective", with people agreeing and arguing
  story: an argument
  subject: NONE. Not coinable.

  posts: "K-pop industry faces criticism", with reactions
  story: an argument
  subject: NONE. Not coinable. A named idol in these posts is still not the subject of
           them; fans reposting that idol's own moment would be.

  posts: "Apple and LG leak new HomeKit features", reposted by tech accounts
  story: product news
  subject: NONE. Not coinable, even though Apple is a specific name: a list of features
           has no one thing at its centre.
  posts: a new AI model everyone is posting about by name, its answers screenshotted
  subject: the model, named as the posts name it. Coinable.

  posts: "Stray Kids unveil single" passed around as an announcement
  story: a press release
  subject: NONE. Not coinable.
  posts: fan accounts reposting a Stray Kids member's photos, fancams and streaming goals
  story: fan content
  subject: NONE. Not coinable. Fans posting their idol is fandom, not a meme.
  posts: the plush toy a Stray Kids member threw into the crowd, edited into jokes by
         many accounts
  subject: the toy, named as the posts name it. Coinable.

  posts: a singer replies to a politician's post about her, with reactions
  story: celebrity and political news
  subject: NONE. Not coinable, however sharp the reply.

When the story is clear and the subject is not, say there is no subject. A story with no
subject is a normal outcome. It is not a failure to be avoided — and neither is a subject:
when one nameable thing is at the centre, say so.
