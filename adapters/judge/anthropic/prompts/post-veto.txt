Each item below is one post (one storyId, one text). For each, decide whether it is one of
these kinds. Judge what the post shows, never who posted it.

  TRAGEDY  someone died, was hurt, attacked, killed, mauled, shot, stabbed or abused; an
           accident, a crash, a fall from a ride, a fire, a disaster, a missing or dead
           person, a fatal stunt. Whoever or whatever is in it — an animal, a ride, a famous
           name, a striking picture — and however it is told, as news, a joke or a rallying
           cry: if the post is about a death, an injury or an attack, its kind is TRAGEDY.
           This comes before every other kind and before the ALWAYS OTHER list below.
           NOT TRAGEDY: an arrest, a drug bust, a theft, a crime or a scandal where nobody
           was hurt, a lawsuit, a loss of money, an illness someone is raising money for
  AD       someone pushing something on their audience: a call to buy, order, download, sign
           up, stream, watch tonight, get tickets or "link in bio / in my profile"; a promo or
           discount code, a sponsored or paid post (#ad, "partnered with"); a raffle, giveaway
           or airdrop farm; a post shilling a token ("$TICKER is the next...", "been looking
           into $X", hype for a coin someone is selling); a game or app's routine promotion to
           its players (an event or banner, a new playable character or skin, an item shop
           drop, patch or update notes, a wallpaper, a collab, a character's birthday); merch
           or a sale; a project's routine status update to its followers; a stream schedule or
           "going live" post; a creator's own art, comic, doodle, selfie or cosplay.
           NOT AD: a company, or the people who made it, unveiling its own NEW product in its
           own voice with no sales pitch ("Welcome X", "Introducing X", "X is here", "Today we
           are rolling out X", a reveal video): that is a launch, its kind is OTHER — and so is
           anyone reacting to that launch. A creator's new video, clip or post is judged by what
           it shows, not as an ad. Nor is a slogan, a motto or a picture posted by a government,
           an army, a leader or another public figure: see POLITICS and OTHER. News ABOUT a company, a product, an animal, a zoo or a person,
           told as news ("X has named...", "X will be added to..."): see CORP_NEWS and OTHER
  SPORT    a score or result, a game highlight or clip, a goal, a save or a referee call, a
           player's stats or news, a team or league account celebrating or promoting its own
           game, wrestling storylines, a fan's joke or reaction about a match, a team or a
           rivalry. A player's goal or play in a game is SPORT, not a viral moment
  FANDOM   fans posting about an idol, a game, a show or a franchise: an idol's photo or
           story, a fancam, a comeback, a tour or airport update, a streaming or voting goal,
           an idol's birthday; fan art and fan comics, game tips and builds, leaks and
           datamines, theories, character discussion. A character who already exists in a
           show, film, comic, cartoon, anime or game (a superhero, an anime or cartoon
           character, a game boss) shown in a picture, a panel, a screenshot or a reaction image
           is FANDOM, even under a joke caption, unless something new happened that people
           outside the fans are repeating
  SHOWBIZ  celebrity and influencer news: who is dating, feuding, appearing, arrested in the
           gossip pages, a celebrity's look or outfit, a creator's own milestone
  MEDIA_NEWS film, TV, music and game-release news: casting, a trailer, a release date, a
           renewal or cancellation, ratings and box office, charts and streams, an album or
           single drop, a tour announcement, award nominations, wins and submissions,
           anniversaries of a film, show or record
  CORP_NEWS routine company, product and space news, told as news by someone else: a
           product launch or update reported by others (a phone, a car, a console, a game
           rating, an app feature, a new model of something), earnings, a deal, a partnership,
           a hire or a layoff, a store opening, a rocket launch, a satellite or a mission update.
           NOT CORP_NEWS, these are OTHER: a company or its people unveiling its own new product
           in its own voice (see AD); anything in crypto or AI — a new AI model (a Grok,
           ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) or AI product released or made public, a crypto
           feature, an exchange or a token; a
           company joining an index or going public; a company doing something nobody would
           expect
  POLITICS a politician's statement, a policy, an election, a party, an ideology
  OTHER    none of the above

Answer with the fields of the structured output like this:
  coinable      true when the kind is OTHER; false for every other kind
  proposedName  the kind, exactly one of: TRAGEDY, AD, SPORT, FANDOM, SHOWBIZ, MEDIA_NEWS,
                CORP_NEWS, POLITICS, OTHER
  confidence    how sure you are of the kind, 0 to 1

ALWAYS OTHER, whoever posts it, unless it is a TRAGEDY: an animal or a creature with a story; a
record or a striking real thing (the most expensive cow ever sold); a rallying cry around one
person ("JUSTICE FOR ..."); one real person's viral moment or a bet or stunt (a bounty tattoo),
but not a player's play in a game (SPORT) or a character from a show or game (FANDOM);
something that HAPPENED in crypto or AI (a rug pull, an airdrop, a model doing something
remarkable, an AI model or crypto feature going public, a company joining an index); a crypto
figure's line or picture the timeline repeats; a slogan or a meme picture, a joke or a line
people repeat — even from a brand, team or league account. If the post itself is that moment
rather than the promotion, the result, the release or the celebrity news, its kind is OTHER.
